<Layer kind="intuizione">

<div data-note-id="massimo-trasferimento-potenza-intuizione-1">

If the load is too small it "short-circuits" the source, if it is too large it lets little current through: in between there is an optimal compromise. In sinusoidal steady state the source reactance must also be cancelled by an opposite reactance: hence the conjugate.

</div>

</Layer>

<Layer kind="formalismo">

<div data-note-id="massimo-trasferimento-potenza-teoria-1">

An equivalent source $\dot V_{th}$, $Z_{th}=R_{th}+jX_{th}$ feeds a load $Z_L=R_L+jX_L$. The absorbed active power is maximum for $Z_L=Z_{th}^*$ (**conjugate matching**) and equals

$$
P_{max}=\frac{|V_{th}|^2}{8R_{th}}\ \ (V_{th}\text{ peak}),\qquad P_{max}=\frac{|V_{th}|^2}{4R_{th}}\ \ (V_{th}\text{ RMS}).
$$

</div>

<div data-note-id="massimo-trasferimento-potenza-teoria-2">

With a purely resistive load the maximum is at $R_L=|Z_{th}|$. The efficiency (load power / power delivered by $V_{th}$) at matching is $50\%$. With an ideal transformer of ratio $n$ the reflected load is $n^2R_L$ and matching requires $n^2R_L=R_{th}$.

</div>

<details class="proof" data-note-id="massimo-trasferimento-potenza-dimostrazione-1">
<summary>Proof: Maximum active power transfer</summary>

**Statement.** $P_L$ is maximum for $Z_L=Z_{th}^*$, with $P_{max}=|V_{th}|^2/(8R_{th})$ (peak).

1. Current: $\dot I=\dfrac{\dot V_{th}}{(R_{th}+R_L)+j(X_{th}+X_L)}$. Active power in the load (peak): $P_L=\tfrac12R_L|\dot I|^2=\dfrac{|V_{th}|^2R_L}{2\left[(R_{th}+R_L)^2+(X_{th}+X_L)^2\right]}$.
2. For every fixed $R_L$ the denominator is minimum if $X_L=-X_{th}$ (the total reactance vanishes), where $P_L=\dfrac{|V_{th}|^2R_L}{2(R_{th}+R_L)^2}$.
3. Differentiating with respect to $R_L$: $\dfrac{d}{dR_L}\dfrac{R_L}{(R_{th}+R_L)^2}=\dfrac{R_{th}-R_L}{(R_{th}+R_L)^3}=0\Rightarrow R_L=R_{th}$ (a maximum: the derivative goes from positive to negative).
4. Hence $Z_L=R_{th}-jX_{th}=Z_{th}^*$ and $P_{max}=\dfrac{|V_{th}|^2R_{th}}{2(2R_{th})^2}=\dfrac{|V_{th}|^2}{8R_{th}}$. With RMS values ($|V|^2=|V_m|^2/2$) $P_{max}=|V_{th}|^2/(4R_{th})$. $\square$

</details>

<Layer kind="esempio">

<div class="worked" data-note-id="massimo-trasferimento-potenza-esempio-1">

### Conjugate matching

$V_{th}=8\,\mathrm V$ (peak), $Z_{th}=4+3j\,\Omega$.

**Solution.**

1. $Z_L=4-3j\,\Omega$; $P_{max}=\dfrac{64}{8\cdot4}=2\,\mathrm W$.

</div>

<div class="worked" data-note-id="massimo-trasferimento-potenza-esempio-2">

### With an ideal transformer

$V_{th}=8\,\mathrm V$ (peak), $R_{th}=8\,\Omega$, load $R_L=2\,\Omega$.

**Solution.**

1. $n^2R_L=R_{th}\Rightarrow n^2=4$, $n=2$.
2. $P_{max}=\dfrac{64}{8\cdot8}=1\,\mathrm W$: it does not depend on $n$ (only on the matching).

</div>

<div class="worked" data-note-id="massimo-trasferimento-potenza-esempio-3">

### Purely resistive load

$Z_{th}=3+4j\,\Omega$, resistive load.

**Solution.**

1. $R_L=|Z_{th}|=5\,\Omega$ (not $3$): with $X_L=0$ the reactance $X_{th}$ cannot be cancelled.

</div>

</Layer>

<Layer kind="errori">

<div data-note-id="massimo-trasferimento-potenza-errore-1">

- **Wrong:** Using $Z_L=Z_{th}$ instead of the conjugate.
- **Right:** $Z_L=Z_{th}^*$ is needed to cancel the total reactance.

</div>

<div data-note-id="massimo-trasferimento-potenza-errore-2">

- **Wrong:** Confusing $/(8R_{th})$ (peak) and $/(4R_{th})$ (RMS).
- **Right:** State whether $V_{th}$ is a peak or an RMS value.

</div>

</Layer>
