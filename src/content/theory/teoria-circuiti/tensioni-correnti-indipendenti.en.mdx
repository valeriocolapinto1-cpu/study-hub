<Layer kind="intuizione">

<div data-note-id="tensioni-correnti-indipendenti-intuizione-1">

A circuit with $b$ branches has $2b$ unknowns, but Kirchhoff's laws "tie" many of them together. Knowing the tree-branch voltages gives all the voltages, and knowing the cotree currents gives all the currents: this is the idea behind the node and mesh methods.

</div>

</Layer>

<Layer kind="formalismo">

<div data-note-id="tensioni-correnti-indipendenti-teoria-1">

The $b$ branch voltages are not independent: they must satisfy KVL ($b-n+1$ constraints). **$n-1$ independent voltages** remain: for instance the tree-branch voltages $\mathbf v_t$, or the node voltages $\mathbf e$ with respect to the reference ($\mathbf v=A^{\mathsf T}\mathbf e$).

</div>

<div data-note-id="tensioni-correnti-indipendenti-teoria-2">

Likewise for currents: KCL gives $n-1$ constraints and **$b-n+1$ independent currents** remain: those of the cotree branches $\mathbf i_c$ (loop currents $\mathbf j$, with $\mathbf i=B^{\mathsf T}\mathbf j$).

</div>

<div data-note-id="tensioni-correnti-indipendenti-teoria-3">

In blocks: $\mathbf v_c=F^{\mathsf T}\mathbf v_t$ (from KVL $B\mathbf v=0$) and $\mathbf i_t=-F\,\mathbf i_c$ (from KCL $Q\mathbf i=0$).

</div>

<details class="proof" data-note-id="tensioni-correnti-indipendenti-dimostrazione-1">
<summary>Proof: All voltages follow from the tree voltages</summary>

1. KVL with $B=[-F^{\mathsf T}\ \ \mathbb1]$ gives $-F^{\mathsf T}\mathbf v_t+\mathbf v_c=0$.
2. Hence $\mathbf v_c=F^{\mathsf T}\mathbf v_t$: the cotree voltages are determined by the tree voltages.
3. The $n-1$ tree voltages are free: there are no further constraints (there are as many KVL equations as fundamental loops, and they have all been used). $\square$

</details>

<Layer kind="esempio">

<div class="worked" data-note-id="tensioni-correnti-indipendenti-esempio-1">

### From the previous triangle

In the triangle of example II-03 (tree $a,b$) with $v_a=5\,\mathrm{V}$, $v_b=3\,\mathrm{V}$: find $v_c$ and, given $i_c=2\,\mathrm{A}$, the currents $i_a,i_b$.

**Solution.**

1. $F^{\mathsf T}=(-1\ \ 1)$: $v_c=-5+3=-2\,\mathrm{V}$ (KVL: $v_a-v_b+v_c=0$ ✓).
2. $\mathbf i_t=-F\,i_c=\begin{pmatrix}1\\-1\end{pmatrix}\cdot2=\begin{pmatrix}2\\-2\end{pmatrix}$: $i_a=2\,\mathrm{A}$, $i_b=-2\,\mathrm{A}$.
3. KCL at node 1: $c$ leaves ($i_c=2$) and $a$ enters ($i_a=2$) ✓.

</div>

</Layer>

<Layer kind="errori">

<div data-note-id="tensioni-correnti-indipendenti-errore-1">

- **Wrong:** Assigning $b$ branch voltages freely.
- **Right:** Only $n-1$ voltages (and $b-n+1$ currents) are independent: the others follow from KVL/KCL.

</div>
