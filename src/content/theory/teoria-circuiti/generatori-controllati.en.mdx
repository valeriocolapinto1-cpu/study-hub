<Layer kind="intuizione">

<div data-note-id="generatori-controllati-intuizione-1">

A controlled source is a "tap" operated by another quantity of the circuit: its output is not fixed from outside but copies, amplified, a voltage or current found elsewhere. That is why it is not a true signal source and stays in place even when the independent sources are switched off.

</div>

</Layer>

<Layer kind="formalismo">

<div data-note-id="generatori-controllati-teoria-1">

A **controlled source** is a two-port: the quantity at one port (input, controlling port) determines the quantity at the other (output). The four linear types:

</div>

<div data-note-id="generatori-controllati-teoria-2">

- **VCVS** (voltage ↔ voltage): $v_2=\mu\,v_1$, $i_1=0$.
- **VCCS** (voltage → current): $i_2=g_m\,v_1$, $i_1=0$.
- **CCVS** (current → voltage): $v_2=r_m\,i_1$, $v_1=0$.
- **CCCS** (current ↔ current): $i_2=\beta\,i_1$, $v_1=0$.

</div>

<div data-note-id="generatori-controllati-teoria-3">

They are _active_ elements (they can deliver net power) and _non-reciprocal_. They are used to model amplifiers, transistors, etc.

</div>

<div data-note-id="generatori-controllati-teoria-4">

**Working rule.** In circuits with controlled sources, when the "independent sources are switched off" (for Thévenin, Norton, zero-input response) the controlled sources stay in the circuit.

</div>

<Layer kind="esempio">

<div class="worked" data-note-id="generatori-controllati-esempio-1">

### VCCS with an upstream divider

A source $V_g=6\,\mathrm{V}$ feeds $R_1=2\,\Omega$ in series with $R_2=4\,\Omega$; the voltage $v_2$ across $R_2$ controls a VCCS with $g_m=0.5\,\mathrm{S}$ feeding $R_3=3\,\Omega$. Find the voltage across $R_3$.

**Solution.**

1. The control is a voltage and draws no current: $v_2 = 6\cdot\dfrac{4}{2+4}=4\,\mathrm{V}$.
2. $i_{out}=g_m v_2=0.5\cdot4=2\,\mathrm{A}$.
3. $v_3=R_3\,i_{out}=3\cdot2=6\,\mathrm{V}$.

</div>

</Layer>

<Layer kind="errori">

<div data-note-id="generatori-controllati-errore-1">

- **Wrong:** Switching off a VCCS as well when finding $R_{th}$ ("it is a source").
- **Right:** Only _independent_ sources are switched off; the controlled one stays and $R_{th}$ is found with the test source $V/I$.

</div>

</Layer>
