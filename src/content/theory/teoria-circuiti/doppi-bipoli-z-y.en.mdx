<Layer kind="intuizione">

<div data-note-id="doppi-bipoli-z-y-intuizione-1">

A linear two-port is "measured" with four experiments: drive one port and look at what happens at both, with the other port open ($Z$ parameters) or shorted ($Y$ parameters). The four numbers obtained are enough to predict its behaviour with any external connection.

</div>

<div data-note-id="doppi-bipoli-z-y-intuizione-2">

The parameters have a physical meaning: $z_{11}$ is the impedance seen from port 1 with port 2 open, $z_{21}$ tells how much voltage appears at port 2 for each ampere injected into port 1. Dually $y_{11}$ is the admittance seen from port 1 with port 2 shorted, $y_{21}$ the current in the short of port 2 for each volt applied to port 1.

</div>

</Layer>

<Layer kind="formalismo">

<div data-note-id="doppi-bipoli-z-y-teoria-1">

With currents entering the ports: $\mathbf v=Z\mathbf i$, i.e. $v_1=z_{11}i_1+z_{12}i_2$, $v_2=z_{21}i_1+z_{22}i_2$. _Open-circuit_ parameters: $z_{11}=v_1/i_1|_{i_2=0}$, $z_{21}=v_2/i_1|_{i_2=0}$, $z_{12}=v_1/i_2|_{i_1=0}$, $z_{22}=v_2/i_2|_{i_1=0}$. $\mathbf i=Y\mathbf v$ with _short-circuit_ parameters: $y_{11}=i_1/v_1|_{v_2=0}$ etc. If both exist, $Y=Z^{-1}$.

</div>

<div data-note-id="doppi-bipoli-z-y-teoria-2">

A **reciprocal** network (only $R$, $L$, $C$ and coupled transformers): $z_{12}=z_{21}$, $y_{12}=y_{21}$. With controlled sources it may not be. The ideal transformer has neither $Z$ nor $Y$.

</div>

<div data-note-id="doppi-bipoli-z-y-teoria-3">

**From $Z$ to $Y$.** If $\Delta_z=z_{11}z_{22}-z_{12}z_{21}\neq0$, $Y=Z^{-1}$: $y_{11}=\dfrac{z_{22}}{\Delta_z}$, $y_{12}=-\dfrac{z_{12}}{\Delta_z}$, $y_{21}=-\dfrac{z_{21}}{\Delta_z}$, $y_{22}=\dfrac{z_{11}}{\Delta_z}$. In general $y_{11}\neq1/z_{11}$, because the two parameters are measured in different conditions (port 2 shorted or open).

</div>

<div data-note-id="doppi-bipoli-z-y-teoria-4">

**Input impedance with a load.** Closing port 2 on $Z_L$ ($v_2=-Z_Li_2$) gives $Z_{in}=z_{11}-\dfrac{z_{12}z_{21}}{z_{22}+Z_L}$. With the port open ($Z_L\to\infty$) $Z_{in}=z_{11}$; shorted ($Z_L=0$) $Z_{in}=\Delta_z/z_{22}=1/y_{11}$.

</div>

<div data-note-id="doppi-bipoli-z-y-teoria-5">

**Equivalent circuit.** A reciprocal two-port ($z_{12}=z_{21}$) is equivalent to a T network with branches $z_{11}-z_{12}$ and $z_{22}-z_{12}$ towards the ports and $z_{12}$ as the shunt; a non-reciprocal one needs a controlled source as well.

</div>

<Layer kind="esempio">

<div class="worked" data-note-id="doppi-bipoli-z-y-esempio-1">

### T two-port

$R_a=1$ (port 1), $R_b=2$ (port 2), $R_c=3$ in shunt.

**Solution.**

1. Port 2 open: $z_{11}=R_a+R_c=4$, $z_{21}=R_c=3$.
2. Port 1 open: $z_{22}=R_b+R_c=5$, $z_{12}=R_c=3$.
3. $Z=\begin{pmatrix}4&3\\3&5\end{pmatrix}$ (reciprocal).

</div>

<div class="worked" data-note-id="doppi-bipoli-z-y-esempio-2">

### π two-port

$G_a=1$ (port 1), $G_b=2$ (port 2), $G_c=3$ in series between the upper terminals.

**Solution.**

1. Port 2 shorted: $y_{11}=G_a+G_c=4$, $y_{21}=-G_c=-3$.
2. Port 1 shorted: $y_{22}=G_b+G_c=5$, $y_{12}=-3$.
3. $Y=\begin{pmatrix}4&-3\\-3&5\end{pmatrix}$ and $Z=Y^{-1}=\dfrac1{11}\begin{pmatrix}5&3\\3&4\end{pmatrix}$.

</div>

<div class="worked" data-note-id="doppi-bipoli-z-y-esempio-3">

### Using the matrix

$Z$ as above: $i_1=1$, port 2 open ($i_2=0$).

**Solution.**

1. $v_1=4$, $v_2=3$.

</div>

<div class="worked" data-note-id="doppi-bipoli-z-y-esempio-4">

### A non-reciprocal two-port

Port 1: $R_1=2\,\Omega$ to ground. Port 2: $R_2=4\,\Omega$ to ground and a CCCS injecting the current $3\,i_1$ into the node of port 2. Find $Z$ and $Y$.

**Solution.**

1. Port 2 open: $z_{11}=2\,\Omega$; all the current $3i_1$ flows in $R_2$, so $v_2=12i_1$ and $z_{21}=12\,\Omega$.
2. Port 1 open ($i_1=0$, CCCS off): $z_{12}=0$, $z_{22}=4\,\Omega$. $Z=\begin{pmatrix}2&0\\12&4\end{pmatrix}$, non-reciprocal ($z_{12}\neq z_{21}$).
3. $\Delta_z=8$: $Y=\dfrac18\begin{pmatrix}4&0\\-12&2\end{pmatrix}=\begin{pmatrix}0.5&0\\-1.5&0.25\end{pmatrix}\,\mathrm S$. Check of $y_{21}$: with $v_1=1\,\mathrm V$ and port 2 shorted, $i_1=0.5\,\mathrm A$ and the $1.5\,\mathrm A$ of the CCCS leave the network into the short: $i_2=-1.5\,\mathrm A$ ✓.

</div>

</Layer>

<Layer kind="errori">

<div data-note-id="doppi-bipoli-z-y-errore-1">

- **Wrong:** Measuring $z_{11}$ with port 2 shorted.
- **Right:** $z_{11}$ is measured with port 2 _open_ ($i_2=0$).

</div>

<div data-note-id="doppi-bipoli-z-y-errore-2">

- **Wrong:** Current signs: assuming $i_2$ leaving.
- **Right:** Standard convention: currents entering both ports.

</div>

<div data-note-id="doppi-bipoli-z-y-errore-3">

- **Wrong:** Computing $y_{11}$ as $1/z_{11}$.
- **Right:** $y_{11}=z_{22}/\Delta_z$: it equals $1/z_{11}$ only if $z_{12}z_{21}=0$, as in the non-reciprocal example.

</div>

<div data-note-id="doppi-bipoli-z-y-errore-4">

- **Wrong:** Computing $z_{21}$ by measuring the port-2 voltage with port 2 loaded.
- **Right:** All $Z$ parameters are measured with the other port open: with a load the current $i_2$ is no longer zero and the ratio $v_2/i_1$ is not $z_{21}$.

</div>
