<Layer kind="intuizione">

<div data-note-id="doppi-bipoli-z-y-intuizione-1">

A linear two-port is "measured" with four experiments: drive one port and look at what happens at both, with the other port open ($Z$ parameters) or shorted ($Y$ parameters). The four numbers obtained are enough to predict its behaviour with any external connection.

</div>

</Layer>

<Layer kind="formalismo">

<div data-note-id="doppi-bipoli-z-y-teoria-1">

With currents entering the ports: $\mathbf v=Z\mathbf i$, i.e. $v_1=z_{11}i_1+z_{12}i_2$, $v_2=z_{21}i_1+z_{22}i_2$. _Open-circuit_ parameters: $z_{11}=v_1/i_1|_{i_2=0}$, $z_{21}=v_2/i_1|_{i_2=0}$, $z_{12}=v_1/i_2|_{i_1=0}$, $z_{22}=v_2/i_2|_{i_1=0}$. $\mathbf i=Y\mathbf v$ with _short-circuit_ parameters: $y_{11}=i_1/v_1|_{v_2=0}$ etc. If both exist, $Y=Z^{-1}$.

</div>

<div data-note-id="doppi-bipoli-z-y-teoria-2">

A **reciprocal** network (only $R$, $L$, $C$ and coupled transformers): $z_{12}=z_{21}$, $y_{12}=y_{21}$. With controlled sources it may not be. The ideal transformer has neither $Z$ nor $Y$.

</div>

<Layer kind="esempio">

<div class="worked" data-note-id="doppi-bipoli-z-y-esempio-1">

### T two-port

$R_a=1$ (port 1), $R_b=2$ (port 2), $R_c=3$ in shunt.

**Solution.**

1. Port 2 open: $z_{11}=R_a+R_c=4$, $z_{21}=R_c=3$.
2. Port 1 open: $z_{22}=R_b+R_c=5$, $z_{12}=R_c=3$.
3. $Z=\begin{pmatrix}4&3\\3&5\end{pmatrix}$ (reciprocal).

</div>

<div class="worked" data-note-id="doppi-bipoli-z-y-esempio-2">

### π two-port

$G_a=1$ (port 1), $G_b=2$ (port 2), $G_c=3$ in series between the upper terminals.

**Solution.**

1. Port 2 shorted: $y_{11}=G_a+G_c=4$, $y_{21}=-G_c=-3$.
2. Port 1 shorted: $y_{22}=G_b+G_c=5$, $y_{12}=-3$.
3. $Y=\begin{pmatrix}4&-3\\-3&5\end{pmatrix}$ and $Z=Y^{-1}=\dfrac1{11}\begin{pmatrix}5&3\\3&4\end{pmatrix}$.

</div>

<div class="worked" data-note-id="doppi-bipoli-z-y-esempio-3">

### Using the matrix

$Z$ as above: $i_1=1$, port 2 open ($i_2=0$).

**Solution.**

1. $v_1=4$, $v_2=3$.

</div>

<Layer kind="errori">

<div data-note-id="doppi-bipoli-z-y-errore-1">

- **Wrong:** Measuring $z_{11}$ with port 2 shorted.
- **Right:** $z_{11}$ is measured with port 2 _open_ ($i_2=0$).

</div>

<div data-note-id="doppi-bipoli-z-y-errore-2">

- **Wrong:** Current signs: assuming $i_2$ leaving.
- **Right:** Standard convention: currents entering both ports.

</div>
