<Layer kind="intuizione">

<div data-note-id="circuiti-risonanti-intuizione-1">

In an RLC circuit, inductor and capacitor exchange energy like a spring and a mass. At the resonant frequency their reactances cancel each other and the circuit responds at its maximum: the smaller the resistance, the "sharper" and higher the resonance.

</div>

</Layer>

<Layer kind="formalismo">

<div data-note-id="circuiti-risonanti-teoria-1">

**Series RLC.** $Z=R+j(\omega L-1/(\omega C))$. At **resonance** $\omega_0=1/\sqrt{LC}$ the reactance is zero, $|Z|$ is minimum and equal to $R$. Quality factor $Q=\dfrac{\omega_0L}{R}=\dfrac{1}{\omega_0RC}$; $3\,\mathrm{dB}$ bandwidth $B=\omega_0/Q=R/L$. At resonance the voltage across $L$ and $C$ is $Q$ times the source voltage.

</div>

<div data-note-id="circuiti-risonanti-teoria-2">

**Parallel RLC.** $Y=G+j(\omega C-1/(\omega L))$: at resonance the admittance is minimum and the impedance maximum ($=R$). $Q=\dfrac{R}{\omega_0L}=\omega_0RC$, $B=\omega_0/Q=1/(RC)$. It is the dual of the series case.

</div>

<details class="proof" data-note-id="circuiti-risonanti-dimostrazione-1">
<summary>Proof: 3 dB bandwidth of the series RLC</summary>

**Statement.** The $3\,\mathrm{dB}$ bandwidth of the current is exactly $B=\omega_2-\omega_1=R/L=\omega_0/Q$.

1. $|I|/|I|_{max}=1/\sqrt2$ when $|X|=R$, i.e. $\omega L-\dfrac1{\omega C}=\pm R$.
2. Solving $\omega^2LC\mp\omega RC-1=0$: $\omega_{1,2}=\mp\dfrac{R}{2L}+\sqrt{\left(\dfrac{R}{2L}\right)^2+\dfrac1{LC}}$ (positive roots).
3. Subtracting: $\omega_2-\omega_1=\dfrac{R}{L}=\dfrac{\omega_0}{Q}$ (the two positive roots differ by exactly $R/L$). $\square$

</details>

<Layer kind="esempio">

<div class="worked" data-note-id="circuiti-risonanti-esempio-1">

### Numerical series RLC

$R=1\,\Omega$, $L=1\,\mathrm H$, $C=\tfrac14\,\mathrm F$.

**Solution.**

1. $\omega_0=\dfrac{1}{\sqrt{1/4}}=2\,\mathrm{rad/s}$, $Q=\dfrac{2\cdot1}{1}=2$, $B=\dfrac{R}{L}=1\,\mathrm{rad/s}$ ($=\omega_0/Q$ ✓).

</div>

<div class="worked" data-note-id="circuiti-risonanti-esempio-2">

### Parallel RLC

$R=4\,\Omega$, $L=1\,\mathrm H$, $C=1\,\mathrm F$.

**Solution.**

1. $\omega_0=1$, $Q=\dfrac{R}{\omega_0L}=4$, $B=\dfrac{1}{RC}=\tfrac14$.

</div>

<div class="worked" data-note-id="circuiti-risonanti-esempio-3">

### Overvoltage

Series circuit with $Q=10$ fed by $1\,\mathrm V$.

**Solution.**

1. At resonance $|V_C|=|V_L|=Q\cdot1=10\,\mathrm V$.

</div>

</Layer>

<Layer kind="errori">

<div data-note-id="circuiti-risonanti-errore-1">

- **Wrong:** Using $Q=\omega_0L/R$ for the parallel circuit as well.
- **Right:** In the parallel circuit $Q=R/(\omega_0L)=\omega_0RC$.

</div>

</Layer>
