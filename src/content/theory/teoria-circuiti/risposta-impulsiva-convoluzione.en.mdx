<Layer kind="intuizione">

<div data-note-id="risposta-impulsiva-convoluzione-intuizione-1">

An instantaneous "kick" at the input makes the circuit ring according to its nature: that response is its fingerprint. Any input can be seen as a train of successive kicks, and convolution adds up their responses.

</div>

</Layer>

<Layer kind="formalismo">

<div data-note-id="risposta-impulsiva-convoluzione-teoria-1">

The **impulse response** $h(t)$ is the output for $x=\delta(t)$ with zero initial conditions. For a causal system $h(t)=0$ for $t<0$. The output for any input (zero initial conditions) is the **convolution** $y(t)=\int_{0}^{t}h(\tau)x(t-\tau)\,d\tau$. The step response is the integral of $h$.

</div>

<div data-note-id="risposta-impulsiva-convoluzione-teoria-2">

The link with the network function is $h(t)=\mathcal L^{-1}\{H(s)\}$: the poles of $H$ determine the modes of $h$ and hence stability (if $h$ is absolutely integrable the system is stable).

</div>

<details class="proof" data-note-id="risposta-impulsiva-convoluzione-dimostrazione-1">
<summary>Proof: Impulse response and network function</summary>

**Statement.** $h(t)=\mathcal L^{-1}\{H(s)\}$ and $y=h*x$.

1. For $x=\delta(t)$ we have $X(s)=1$.
2. With zero initial conditions $Y(s)=H(s)X(s)=H(s)$.
3. Inverting, $y=h=\mathcal L^{-1}\{H\}$.
4. For any input $Y=HX$; since $\mathcal L\{h*x\}=HX$ (convolution property), $y=h*x$. $\square$

</details>

<Layer kind="esempio">

<div class="worked" data-note-id="risposta-impulsiva-convoluzione-esempio-1">

### Impulse response of an RC

$H=\dfrac{1}{s+1}$.

**Solution.**

1. $h(t)=e^{-t}u_{-1}(t)$.

</div>

<div class="worked" data-note-id="risposta-impulsiva-convoluzione-esempio-2">

### Step response

Same system, input $u_{-1}$.

**Solution.**

1. $y=\int_0^th=1-e^{-t}$ for $t>0$.

</div>

<div class="worked" data-note-id="risposta-impulsiva-convoluzione-esempio-3">

### Rectangular pulse

Same system, input $1$ for $0<t<1$.

**Solution.**

1. $0<t<1$: $y=1-e^{-t}$.
2. $t>1$: $y=(1-e^{-1})e^{-(t-1)}$ (continuity at $t=1$).

</div>

</Layer>

<Layer kind="errori">

<div data-note-id="risposta-impulsiva-convoluzione-errore-1">

- **Wrong:** Deriving $h$ from the step response without differentiating.
- **Right:** $h=\dfrac{d}{dt}y_{step}$.

</div>
