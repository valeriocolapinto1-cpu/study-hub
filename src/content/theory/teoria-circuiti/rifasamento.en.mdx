<Layer kind="intuizione">

<div data-note-id="rifasamento-intuizione-1">

A motor (inductive load) also draws reactive power from the grid, which drives current through the cables without doing any work. A capacitor in parallel supplies that reactive power "locally": the grid sees a more resistive load, the current drops and so do the losses.

</div>

</Layer>

<Layer kind="formalismo">

<div data-note-id="rifasamento-teoria-1">

An inductive load with $P$ and $\cos\varphi_1$ absorbs $Q_1=P\tan\varphi_1$. Connecting a capacitor $C$ in parallel, the load voltage does not change and the capacitor absorbs $Q_C=-\omega CV^2$. The total reactive power becomes $Q_2=Q_1-\omega CV^2=P\tan\varphi_2$, with $\varphi_2$ the new phase angle.

</div>

<div data-note-id="rifasamento-teoria-2">

$$
C=\frac{P(\tan\varphi_1-\tan\varphi_2)}{\omega V^2}\quad(V\text{ RMS}).
$$

The goal is to reduce the current (and hence the losses) for the same $P$: $I=P/(V\cos\varphi)$.

</div>

<Layer kind="esempio">

<div class="worked" data-note-id="rifasamento-esempio-1">

### Full correction

$P=100\,\mathrm W$, $V=10\,\mathrm V$, $\omega=10\,\mathrm{rad/s}$, $\cos\varphi_1=0.8$ (inductive), $\cos\varphi_2=1$.

**Solution.**

1. $\tan\varphi_1=3/4$, $\tan\varphi_2=0$.
2. $C=\dfrac{100\cdot0.75}{10\cdot100}=0.075\,\mathrm F$.
3. Check: $Q_1=75\,\mathrm{var}$, $Q_C=-\omega CV^2=-10\cdot0.075\cdot100=-75$: zero total reactive power. ✓

</div>

<div class="worked" data-note-id="rifasamento-esempio-2">

### Partial correction

Same load, but $\cos\varphi_2=12/13$ ($\tan\varphi_2=5/12$) is required.

**Solution.**

1. $C=\dfrac{100(3/4-5/12)}{1000}=\dfrac{100\cdot1/3}{1000}=\tfrac1{30}\approx0.0333\,\mathrm F$.

</div>

<div class="worked" data-note-id="rifasamento-esempio-3">

### Current before and after

Same load: compare the RMS currents before and after full correction.

**Solution.**

1. Before: $I_1=P/(V\cos\varphi_1)=100/(10\cdot0.8)=12.5\,\mathrm A$. After: $I_2=100/10=10\,\mathrm A$.

</div>

</Layer>

<Layer kind="errori">

<div data-note-id="rifasamento-errore-1">

- **Wrong:** Connecting a correction inductor (instead of a capacitor) or the capacitor in series.
- **Right:** The capacitor goes in parallel with the inductive load.

</div>
