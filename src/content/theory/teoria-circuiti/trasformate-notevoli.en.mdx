<Layer kind="intuizione">

<div data-note-id="trasformate-notevoli-intuizione-1">

With a dozen transforms by heart and the properties you can rebuild almost everything you need. A "piecewise" signal is split into steps switched on and off at the right time: a delay in time becomes a factor $e^{-sT}$.

</div>

</Layer>

<Layer kind="formalismo">

<div data-note-id="trasformate-notevoli-teoria-1">

| $x(t)$ ($t>0$)        | $X(s)$                      |
| --------------------- | --------------------------- |
| $\delta(t)$           | $1$                         |
| $u_{-1}(t)$           | $1/s$                       |
| $t$                   | $1/s^2$                     |
| $t^n/n!$              | $1/s^{n+1}$                 |
| $e^{-at}$             | $1/(s+a)$                   |
| $te^{-at}$            | $1/(s+a)^2$                 |
| $\cos\omega t$        | $s/(s^2+\omega^2)$          |
| $\sin\omega t$        | $\omega/(s^2+\omega^2)$     |
| $e^{-at}\cos\omega t$ | $(s+a)/((s+a)^2+\omega^2)$  |
| $e^{-at}\sin\omega t$ | $\omega/((s+a)^2+\omega^2)$ |

</div>

<div data-note-id="trasformate-notevoli-teoria-2">

A piecewise signal is written with delayed steps: the rectangular pulse $A[u_{-1}(t)-u_{-1}(t-T)]$ has transform $A\,\dfrac{1-e^{-sT}}{s}$.

</div>

<details class="proof" data-note-id="trasformate-notevoli-dimostrazione-1">
<summary>Proof: Transform of $e^{-at}$ and of the cosine</summary>

1. $\int_0^\infty e^{-at}e^{-st}dt=\left[-\dfrac{e^{-(s+a)t}}{s+a}\right]_0^\infty=\dfrac{1}{s+a}$ (for $\mathrm{Re}\,s>-a$).
2. $\cos\omega t=\tfrac12(e^{j\omega t}+e^{-j\omega t})$: $\mathcal L=\tfrac12\left(\dfrac1{s-j\omega}+\dfrac1{s+j\omega}\right)=\dfrac{s}{s^2+\omega^2}$. $\square$

</details>

<Layer kind="esempio">

<div class="worked" data-note-id="trasformate-notevoli-esempio-1">

### Linear combination

$x(t)=3e^{-2t}+4\sin3t$.

**Solution.**

1. $X(s)=\dfrac{3}{s+2}+\dfrac{12}{s^2+9}$.

</div>

<div class="worked" data-note-id="trasformate-notevoli-esempio-2">

### Rectangular pulse

$x=3$ for $0<t<2$, zero elsewhere.

**Solution.**

1. $x=3[u_{-1}(t)-u_{-1}(t-2)]$, $X(s)=\dfrac{3(1-e^{-2s})}{s}$.

</div>

<div class="worked" data-note-id="trasformate-notevoli-esempio-3">

### Truncated ramp

$x=t$ for $0<t<1$, zero elsewhere.

**Solution.**

1. $t\,u_{-1}(t)-t\,u_{-1}(t-1)$ and $t\,u_{-1}(t-1)=(t-1)u_{-1}(t-1)+u_{-1}(t-1)$.
2. $X(s)=\dfrac1{s^2}-e^{-s}\left(\dfrac1{s^2}+\dfrac1s\right)$.

</div>

</Layer>

<Layer kind="errori">

<div data-note-id="trasformate-notevoli-errore-1">

- **Wrong:** Transforming $\cos\omega t$ as $\omega/(s^2+\omega^2)$.
- **Right:** The cosine gives $s/(s^2+\omega^2)$, the sine $\omega/(s^2+\omega^2)$.

</div>
