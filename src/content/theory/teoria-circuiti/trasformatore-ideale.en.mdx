<Layer kind="intuizione">

<div data-note-id="trasformatore-ideale-intuizione-1">

The ideal transformer is an electrical "lever": it multiplies voltage by $n$ and divides current by $n$, so their product (the power) is unchanged. A load seen "through the lever" looks larger or smaller by a factor $n^2$.

</div>

</Layer>

<Layer kind="formalismo">

<div data-note-id="trasformatore-ideale-teoria-1">

The **ideal transformer** with ratio $n$ is a linear, memoryless two-port. With the passive sign convention on both ports:

$$
v_1 = n\,v_2,\qquad i_1 = -\frac{i_2}{n}.
$$

</div>

<div data-note-id="trasformatore-ideale-teoria-2">

It is not an inductor: it also works in DC and it is a limiting model (perfect coupling, infinite inductances, no losses).

</div>

<div data-note-id="trasformatore-ideale-teoria-3">

**Properties.** The total absorbed power is zero at every instant; a load $Z_L$ at port 2 is seen from port 1 as $n^2 Z_L$ (with $n$ defined as above).

</div>

<details class="proof" data-note-id="trasformatore-ideale-dimostrazione-1">
<summary>Proof: The ideal transformer absorbs no power and reflects impedances</summary>

**Statement.** With $v_1=nv_2$ and $i_1=-i_2/n$: (a) $p_1+p_2=0$; (b) if $v_2=-Z_L\,i_2$ then $v_1/i_1=n^2Z_L$.

1. (a) $p=v_1 i_1+v_2 i_2 = (n v_2)(-i_2/n)+v_2 i_2 = -v_2 i_2+v_2 i_2 = 0$.
2. (b) Port 2 is closed on $Z_L$; with $i_2$ entering the transformer, the load current is $-i_2$, hence $v_2=Z_L(-i_2)$.
3. $Z_{in}=\dfrac{v_1}{i_1}=\dfrac{n\,v_2}{-i_2/n}=n^2\,\dfrac{v_2}{-i_2}=n^2 Z_L$. $\square$

</details>

<Layer kind="esempio">

<div class="worked" data-note-id="trasformatore-ideale-esempio-1">

### Transformer with a resistive load

$n=2$, load $R_L=3\,\Omega$ at port 2; at port 1 a $24\,\mathrm{V}$ source in series with $12\,\Omega$. Find $v_2$ and the load power.

**Solution.**

1. $R_{in}=n^2R_L=12\,\Omega$.
2. Primary loop: $24=12\,i_1+12\,i_1\Rightarrow i_1=1\,\mathrm{A}$, $v_1=12\,\mathrm{V}$.
3. $v_2=v_1/n=6\,\mathrm{V}$, $i_2=-n\,i_1=-2\,\mathrm{A}$ (the load current is $2\,\mathrm{A}$).
4. $P_L=6\cdot2=12\,\mathrm{W}=v_1 i_1$: power is conserved. ✓

</div>

</Layer>

<Layer kind="errori">

<div data-note-id="trasformatore-ideale-errore-1">

- **Wrong:** Using $i_1=i_2/n$ with both currents entering.
- **Right:** With the passive convention on both ports, $i_1=-i_2/n$ (the minus sign compensates for the directions).

</div>
