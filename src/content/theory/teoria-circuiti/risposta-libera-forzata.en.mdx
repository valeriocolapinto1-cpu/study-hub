<Layer kind="intuizione">

<div data-note-id="risposta-libera-forzata-intuizione-1">

The response of a circuit has two "causes": the energy it already stored (initial conditions) and what reaches it from outside (inputs). By linearity the two effects can be computed separately and then added.

</div>

</Layer>

<Layer kind="formalismo">

<div data-note-id="risposta-libera-forzata-teoria-1">

**Zero-input** (free) response: zero input, non-zero initial conditions (it depends only on the natural modes, i.e. on the poles). **Zero-state** (forced) response: zero initial conditions and non-zero input. In a linear system the complete response is the sum $y=y_{zi}+y_{zs}$.

</div>

<div data-note-id="risposta-libera-forzata-teoria-2">

With Laplace: in the transformed circuit set the initial conditions to zero (zero-state) or the inputs to zero (zero-input).

</div>

<Formula id="y-libera-forzata" />

</Layer>

<Layer kind="esempio">

<div class="worked" data-note-id="risposta-libera-forzata-esempio-1">

### RC with a step

$R=2$, $C=\tfrac12$, $v_C(0)=2$, input $10u_{-1}$.

**Solution.**

1. Zero-input: $v_C=2e^{-t}$.
2. Zero-state: $10(1-e^{-t})$.
3. Sum: $10-8e^{-t}$ ✓.

</div>

<div class="worked" data-note-id="risposta-libera-forzata-esempio-2">

### Zero-input only

RL with $R=4$, $L=2$, $i(0)=3$, sources off.

**Solution.**

1. $i=3e^{-2t}$.

</div>

<div class="worked" data-note-id="risposta-libera-forzata-esempio-3">

### Zero-state only

$H=\dfrac1{s+1}$ with $x=u_{-1}$, zero initial conditions.

**Solution.**

1. $Y=\dfrac1{s(s+1)}\Rightarrow y=1-e^{-t}$.

</div>

</Layer>

<Layer kind="errori">

<div data-note-id="risposta-libera-forzata-errore-1">

- **Wrong:** Attributing the transient to the zero-input response alone.
- **Right:** The zero-state response also contains transient terms ($-10e^{-t}$ above).

</div>

</Layer>
